Add the app to your phone

Apple doesn’t let a website install itself, so Safari does it in a few taps:

  1. Tap the ⋯ button next to the address bar.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Leave Open as Web App switched on.
  5. AddTap Add, top right.
  1. Tap the Share button in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. AddTap Add, top right.

The crest appears on your Home Screen. Open the app from there to turn on reminders.

Open this page in Safari. Safari is the sure way to add the app to an iPhone’s Home Screen, and to get its notifications.

  1. Tap Chrome’s menu ⋮, top right.
  2. Tap Install app. Some phones say Add to Home screen, then Install.

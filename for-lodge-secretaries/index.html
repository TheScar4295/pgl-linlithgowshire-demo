For lodge secretariesNew

Send your lodge’s dates once. The Province checks them, and they appear in What’s On, on your lodge’s own page, in the printable syllabus and in every member’s subscribed calendar.

From your diary to everyone’s calendar

  1. Your regular meetings are already there

    Each lodge’s pattern (for example “1st and 3rd Tuesday, September to April”) fills the calendar for the whole season. You only tell us when a date moves or is cancelled.

  2. Send anything special

    Degrees, installations, open days, socials and charity nights: one short form per event, below. You can send a whole syllabus at once as a file instead.

  3. The Province checks it

    A named editor at the Province sees each submission, checks the date, time and venue, and approves it or asks you a question. You get an email either way.

  4. It appears everywhere at once

    Once approved, the entry is in What’s On, on your lodge page and in the season’s syllabus, and it reaches every subscribed phone calendar within a few hours.

  5. Changes work the same way

    Moving or cancelling an event is a new submission for the same entry. Cancelled events stay visible, marked “Cancelled”, so nobody turns up to a dark hall.

When a regular meeting moves or is cancelled

A secretary sends one change for that date. A moved meeting leaves its usual night and carries a note on the new one; a cancelled meeting stays on the calendar, marked cancelled, with the reason. Members who subscribe see either change without doing anything.
